Privacy Policy

At EduAdmin, accessible from your designated portal, we prioritize your privacy. This Privacy Policy documents the types of information collected and how we use it, specifically regarding our Google Drive API integration.

1. Information We Collect

EduAdmin collects basic information necessary to provide academy management features:

  • Account Information: Name, email address, role (teacher, admin, or student), and academy affiliation.
  • App Data: Class names, subjects, attendance logs, syllabus completion, and links to study materials.

2. How We Use Google User Data

EduAdmin allows authorized teachers and administrators to connect their Google Drive accounts. We use this connection strictly for the following purposes:

  • Limited Scope (drive.file): We only request the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file permission. This only allows EduAdmin to upload, read, and manage files and folders created directly by this application (the "EduAdmin" parent folder and sub-folders). We do not read, browse, or access any other files on your Google Drive.
  • File Storage & Folder Management: When a teacher uploads photos, videos, or PDFs, the app creates an organized structure (EduAdmin / Academy Name / Class Name / Subject) and uploads the file directly to your connected Google Drive.
  • Storage Quota Tracking: We query Google Drive storage quota metrics (total space, used space, and remaining space) solely to display a live storage progress bar within the teacher workspace.
  • Public Sharing Authorization: Uploaded materials are marked as viewable via public link so enrolled academy students can view them directly inside the app.

3. Data Transfer, Sharing & Retention

  • No Server-Side Storage of Google Tokens: Google OAuth tokens are processed solely on your device (client-side) and are never sent to or stored on our servers.
  • No Third-Party Sharing: We do not sell, rent, or trade your Google data, files, or personal information with third parties.
  • User-Controlled Deletion: Teachers and administrators can remove any study material at any time. Deleting a material within the app permanently removes the record from EduAdmin and deletes the file from Google Drive.

4. Data Security

All communication with Google Drive and Firestore is encrypted using Industry-Standard TLS/SSL encryption. Authentication is governed directly by Google's secure OAuth 2.0 system.

5. Your Rights & Access Revocation

You can disconnect your Google Drive from the app at any time using the "Switch Drive" or disconnect button, or revoke access directly via your Google Account Security Settings.
