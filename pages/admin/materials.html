Paid Material Approval

How paid access will work after backend integration.

  • Students submit UPI payment details for a paid material.
  • The payment request appears under Fees → Payment Approvals.
  • The admin verifies the payment against the actual UPI or bank record.
  • Only approved payments unlock the purchased material.
  • Pending and rejected payments remain locked.

Payment verification, live notifications, and material access control are not active until the backend is connected.
