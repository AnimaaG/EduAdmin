Approval Rules

These are the intended payment-processing rules.

  • New payment submissions start as Pending Review.
  • Admins must verify the actual received payment before approval.
  • Approved payments grant access only to the associated material or fee.
  • Rejected and pending payments do not grant paid-material access.
  • Repeated transaction references must not approve multiple purchases.
  • Payment decisions should be recorded with the reviewer and timestamp.

Approval actions, secure access control, payment notifications, and audit history are not active until backend services are implemented.
