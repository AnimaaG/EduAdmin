Payment Instructions

Instructions for requesting access to a paid material.

  1. Select a paid material from the list once materials are available.
  2. Review the price and the academy's configured UPI payment details.
  3. Pay using the displayed QR code or UPI details.
  4. Submit the transaction reference through the payment form when available.
  5. Wait for the admin to verify the payment.
  6. Open the material after approval is recorded.
UPI details, QR code, payment submission, and material unlocking are not connected yet. Do not make a payment based on this placeholder page.
